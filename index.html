FuelLog

Your dashboard says one number, the brochure says another - but nobody tracks what your car actually does between fill-ups. A slow drop in efficiency is the earliest sign of an engine problem, and it costs you money every week before you notice.

FuelLog takes ten seconds at the pump: date, odometer, liters, cost. From the second fill-up it computes your real km/L per tank, cost per kilometer, and whether your efficiency is trending up, down, or holding steady.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
